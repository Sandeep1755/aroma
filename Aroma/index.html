Our Roots & Heritage

Why Atreyapuram? The Story Behind Tricity's Most Authentic Andhra Cafe.

Tucked along the fertile Godavari delta in Andhra Pradesh lies **Atreyapuram**—a legendary village world-renowned for its artisanal rice paper sweet, the *Pootharekulu*, and centuries-old culinary secrets passed down through generations of home kitchens.

When **Mr. Suryajoe** founded Atreyapuram Aromas, the mission was uncompromising: bring 100% authentic, unadulterated South Indian cuisine to Zirakpur, Chandigarh, and Mohali. We source our cold-pressed sesame oil, Guntur chillies, Kandi podi, and A2 cow ghee directly from Godavari farmers.
